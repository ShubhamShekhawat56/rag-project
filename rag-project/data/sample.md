# Northwind Labs Employee Handbook

Welcome to Northwind Labs. This handbook explains how we work, what you can expect from us, and what we expect from you. It applies to all full-time employees based in India.

## Annual Leave

Every full-time employee receives 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Leave accrues monthly at two days per month. Up to 5 unused days may be carried over to the next year, but carried-over days expire on March 31. Leave requests of more than three consecutive days should be submitted at least two weeks in advance through the HR portal and approved by your manager.

## Parental Leave

The primary caregiver of a new child is entitled to 26 weeks of fully paid parental leave. The secondary caregiver is entitled to 4 weeks of fully paid leave. Parental leave may be taken in one block or split into two blocks within the first 12 months after the child's birth or adoption. Employees must notify HR at least 60 days before the expected start date wherever possible.

## Remote Work Policy

Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week. Everyone is expected to be available during core hours of 11:00 to 16:00 IST, regardless of location. Team meetings are held on Tuesdays and Thursdays and are in person unless the meeting invite says otherwise. Employees who wish to work remotely from another city for more than two weeks need written approval from their department head.

## Expense Reimbursement

Expense claims must be submitted within 30 days of the purchase date, with receipts attached, through the finance portal. While travelling for work, meals are reimbursed up to 1,500 rupees per day. Any single expense above 10,000 rupees requires prior approval from the department head. Alcohol, personal entertainment, and fines are never reimbursable. Approved claims are paid with the next monthly payroll run.

## Learning and Development

Each employee has a learning budget of 40,000 rupees per year that can be spent on courses, books, certifications, and conferences. Unused budget does not roll over. Employees who attend a paid conference are asked to share a short summary with their team within two weeks of returning.

## Information Security

All passwords must be at least 14 characters long, and two-factor authentication is mandatory on every company account. Laptops must use full-disk encryption and lock automatically after five minutes of inactivity. Any suspected security incident, including a lost device or a phishing email that was clicked, must be reported within 2 hours to security@northwind.example. Customer data may never be copied to personal devices or personal cloud storage.

## Data Handling and AI Usage

Company source code, internal docs, and customer records must stay within approved company workspaces. Employees must not paste confidential company or customer data into unapproved external tools or public AI prompts. All company devices must be returned to IT on or before the last working day.

## Onboarding

New joiners follow a 90-day onboarding plan. Each new joiner is paired with an onboarding buddy for the first three months. Formal check-ins with the manager take place on day 30, day 60, and day 90. During the first week, new joiners complete security training and set up their development environment. The probation period is three months and is confirmed in writing at the day-90 check-in.

## Performance Reviews

Performance is reviewed twice a year, in April and in October. Reviews combine self-assessment, peer feedback from at least three colleagues, and a manager assessment. Salary revisions are decided once a year, after the October review cycle, and take effect from the following January.
